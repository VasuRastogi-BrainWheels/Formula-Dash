# 🏎️ Car Racing Game

A simple, fast-paced 3D car racing game where you compete against 4 other players to reach the top! Race through the track, dodge obstacles, and use various items to gain the upper hand.

## ✨ Features

* **5-Player Racing:** Compete head-to-head against 4 other racers.
* **Powerups:** Grab special items on the track to gain an advantage over your rivals.
* **Coins:** Collect coins scattered across the course.
* **Boosters:** Hit speed pads to leave the competition in the dust.

## 🎮 Controls

### **PC / Keyboard**

* **W:** Accelerate
* **S:** Brake / Reverse
* **Left / Right Arrow Keys:** Steer

### **Mobile / Touch**

* **A Button:** Accelerate
* **B Button:** Brake / Reverse
